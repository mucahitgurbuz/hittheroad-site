About

HitTheRoad is an AI-powered road trip planner. Tell us where you're going — or tap Surprise me for weekend loop ideas from your doorstep — and we build a complete, drivable itinerary in under a minute: real stops, day-by-day timing, and an honest cost estimate.

The app learns your travel taste from quick swipe cards, adapts routes to your vehicle (camper van, EV, motorbike or car), guides you live on the road, and turns every finished trip into a shareable recap and Roadbook badges.

New accounts start with free trips. Planning more? Go Pro for a monthly or yearly subscription, or buy one-time credit packs in-app.

Learn more at gethittheroad.com.

Common questions

How do I contact HitTheRoad support?

Email mucahitgurbuzcompany@gmail.com. Include your device model, your OS version and the screen where the problem happened; a screenshot helps. Replies come from the developer directly.

How do I delete my HitTheRoad account?

Open the app and go to Profile → Account → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and permanent: your account and all data associated with it are removed, and there is no separate request to file.

How do I cancel HitTheRoad Pro?

Cancel from your store account, not from the app: on iPhone in Settings → your name → Subscriptions, and on Android in the Play Store under Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal and Pro stays active until the period you already paid for ends.

Do trip credits expire?

No. Credits you buy one at a time stay on your account until you use them. The monthly allowance that comes with Pro refreshes each billing period.

Which devices does HitTheRoad run on?

iPhone on iOS 16 or later and Android phones on Android 8 or later. The web planner at gethittheroad.com/plan works in any modern browser, with no account needed.
